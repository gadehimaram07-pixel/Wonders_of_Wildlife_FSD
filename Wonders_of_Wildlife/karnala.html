Karnala Bird Sanctuary

Karnala Bird Sanctuary is a famous bird sanctuary in Maharashtra known for its rich birdlife and scenic trekking routes. It is a paradise for birdwatchers and nature lovers.

Location: Maharashtra, India

Famous For: Migratory Birds, Forest Birds, Birdwatching

Habitat Type: Moist deciduous forests and hilly terrain

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Interesting Fact: More than 200 species of birds have been recorded in Karnala Bird Sanctuary.

Conservation Importance: It is important for bird conservation and environmental awareness near urban regions.
