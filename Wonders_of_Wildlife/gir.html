Gir National Park

Gir National Park is the only natural habitat of the Asiatic Lion in the world and is one of India's most important wildlife reserves.

Location: Gujarat, India

Famous For: Asiatic Lions, Leopards, Deer

Habitat Type: Dry deciduous forests and scrublands

Best Time to Visit: December to March

Interesting Fact: Gir is the last remaining home of the Asiatic Lion.
