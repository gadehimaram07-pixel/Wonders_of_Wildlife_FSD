Jim Corbett National Park

Jim Corbett National Park is one of the oldest and most famous national parks in India. It is known for its rich biodiversity and Bengal tiger population.

Location: Uttarakhand, India

Famous For: Bengal Tigers, Elephants, Leopards

Habitat Type: Forests, grasslands, riverine belts

Best Time to Visit: November to June

Interesting Fact: It was the first national park in India and the first to come under Project Tiger.
