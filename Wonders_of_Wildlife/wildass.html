Wild Ass Sanctuary

Wild Ass Sanctuary is a unique wildlife reserve located in the Little Rann of Kutch. It is the only place in India where the Indian Wild Ass is found in large numbers.

Location: Gujarat, India

Famous For: Indian Wild Ass, Desert Fox, Birds

Habitat Type: Salt marshes, grasslands, and desert plains

Best Time to Visit: November to March

Interesting Fact: This sanctuary is one of the largest wildlife sanctuaries in India.

Conservation Importance: It plays a major role in protecting the endangered Indian Wild Ass and desert ecosystem.
