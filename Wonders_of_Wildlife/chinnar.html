Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary

Chinnar Wildlife Sanctuary is a beautiful protected area in Kerala known for its dry thorn forests and rare wildlife species. It is one of the most ecologically important regions in South India.

Location: Kerala, India

Famous For: Grizzled Giant Squirrel, Elephants, Leopards, Birds

Habitat Type: Dry thorn forests, scrublands, and riverine forests

Best Time to Visit: December to April

Interesting Fact: Chinnar is one of the few places in India where the endangered Grizzled Giant Squirrel can be seen.

Conservation Importance: It protects rare dry forest habitats and many endangered species.
