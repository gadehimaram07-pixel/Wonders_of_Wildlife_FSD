Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary

Bondla Wildlife Sanctuary is a small but attractive sanctuary in Goa. It is popular for its lush greenery, educational nature park, and variety of wildlife and birds.

Location: Goa, India

Famous For: Deer, Gaur, Leopards, Birds

Habitat Type: Moist deciduous forests and plantations

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Interesting Fact: Bondla is also known for its mini zoo and botanical garden, making it popular among students and families.

Conservation Importance: It helps preserve Goa’s forest biodiversity and supports wildlife education.
