Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary

Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary is a beautiful protected area in Kerala, famous for elephants and scenic boat safaris on Periyar Lake.

Location: Kerala, India

Famous For: Indian Elephants, Tigers, Birds

Habitat Type: Evergreen and moist deciduous forests

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Interesting Fact: Tourists can often see elephants near the lake during boat rides.
