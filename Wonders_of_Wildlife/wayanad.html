Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary

Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary is one of the most beautiful protected forest regions in Kerala. It is rich in biodiversity and is known for elephants, deer, and a wide variety of birds.

Location: Kerala, India

Famous For: Elephants, Deer, Tigers, Birds

Habitat Type: Moist deciduous forests and evergreen forests

Best Time to Visit: October to May

Interesting Fact: Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary forms an important part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve.

Conservation Importance: It helps protect forest wildlife and supports ecological balance in the Western Ghats.
